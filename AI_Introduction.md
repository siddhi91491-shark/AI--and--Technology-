# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology
that enables computers and machines to perform
tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## Applications of AI

1. Healthcare
2. Education
3. Banking
4. Business
5. Transportation

## Benefits of AI

1. Saves time.
2. Improves productivity.
3. Automates repetitive tasks.
4. Helps in decision-making.
5. Provides faster results.

## Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence is an important
technology that is transforming the modern world.
