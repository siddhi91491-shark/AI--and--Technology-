# Digital Literacy

## What is Digital Literacy?

Digital Literacy refers to the ability to use
digital devices, technologies and the internet
effectively and safely.

## Importance of Digital Literacy

1. Improves communication.
2. Helps in online education.
3. Provides access to information.
4. Improves technical knowledge.
5. Supports career development.

## Types of Digital Skills

1. Computer Skills
2. Internet Skills
3. Communication Skills
4. Online Safety
5. Digital Content Creation

## Advantages of Digital Literacy

1. Saves time.
2. Improves productivity.
3. Makes learning easier.
4. Provides career opportunities.
5. Helps people stay connected.

## Conclusion

Digital Literacy is an essential skill
in today's modern world. It helps people
use technology effectively and responsibly.
